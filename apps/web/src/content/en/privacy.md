## What happens to your data

**Generator, tester (pasted text), server rules, llms.txt and other generators** run entirely in your browser. What you type or paste is not transmitted to us.

**Live robots.txt checks.** When you enter a website, your browser sends the address to our server, which fetches that site's `/robots.txt`. The fetched file and its HTTP details are kept in the server's memory for up to **10 minutes** so that repeated checks are fast, then discarded. We do not write the addresses you check or the fetched files to logs, databases or analytics. To limit abuse, the server counts requests per IP address in memory for one minute; that counter is not stored.

**Server logs.** Our web server may write standard technical logs (time, requested page, status code, anonymised IP address, browser type) for security and troubleshooting. They are deleted after 14 days. Logs for live checks do not contain the checked address.

**Local storage in your browser.** We store your colour-theme choice and whether you dismissed the language suggestion. This stays on your device.

**Analytics.** If enabled, we use Plausible Analytics, which works without cookies and does not collect personal data; it records page views and a few events (“file generated”, preset chosen, “check run”) without the content you entered or the sites you checked. Google Analytics is used only if you give consent.

**Advertising.** There is currently no advertising. If it is introduced, consent will be requested where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland) through a certified consent management platform, and this policy will be updated before it starts.

## Third parties

- **Hosting:** our own servers or a hosting provider acting as processor.
- **Analytics:** Plausible Insights OÜ (EU), only if enabled.

No data is sold or shared for advertising.

## Legal bases and your rights

Processing for live checks and security logs is based on our legitimate interest in providing and securing the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority. Because we keep no user records, there is usually nothing we can identify you by; contact us at {{contact}} with any request.
